The battery supplies energy through a suitable power converter to a dedicated low-power mall load. When the trolley is stopped, the battery can still supply the load until its available energy is depleted.

Real-world safety: Never connect a DIY battery directly to mall mains wiring or a wall socket. Exporting electricity to a building supply requires an approved grid-interconnection inverter, electrical protection, and authorization from qualified professionals. For a student prototype, use an isolated low-voltage DC demonstration load instead.
